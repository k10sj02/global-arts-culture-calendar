# Global Arts & Culture Calendar

An automatically refreshed calendar of film, literature, visual art, performance, experimental music, and multidisciplinary events in selected cities in the United States, Europe, and Mexico.

The project publishes one master ICS feed and separate feeds for the United States, Europe, and Mexico. Events retain their local time zones, official links, locations, categories, stable identifiers, deduplication, and two-run cancellation handling.

Website: https://k10sj02.github.io/global-arts-culture-calendar/

Master subscription: https://k10sj02.github.io/global-arts-culture-calendar/global-culture.ics

Current sources: The Poetry Project, Brown Arts, Chicago Humanities, Harvard Film Archive, George Washington University arts, FringeArts, ArtsATL, the New Orleans Museum of Art, Contemporary Arts Center New Orleans, REDCAT Los Angeles, UC Berkeley arts, Northwest Film Forum in Seattle, ICA London, Matadero Madrid, CCCB Barcelona, silent green Berlin, and INBAL. The site provides dedicated East Coast, West Coast, and Atlanta/New Orleans feeds in addition to the master and regional subscriptions.
